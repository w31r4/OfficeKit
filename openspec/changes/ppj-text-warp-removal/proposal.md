## Why

F-03/F-06 expose preset text warp and literal adjustment guides but source-bound style omission retains the original warp or guides. Their presence lifecycle must match the other direct text-body fields.

## What Changes

- Remove and restore canonical text warp across the existing five body-style owner paths.
- Delete or empty adjustment lists while keeping the preset; require dependent adjustments to be removed with the preset.
- Preserve explicit textNoShape, zero and signed guide values, native source boundaries and editing authority.
- Add an additive deletion intent, shared lifecycle tests and synchronized public field guidance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ppj-text-warp-removal`: Preset and literal adjustment presence lifecycle.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

PPJ schema, additive protocol, native body compiler/normalization, shared lifecycle fixture and presentation references. Updated codec required; complete WordArt rendering and inheritance remain separate gaps.
