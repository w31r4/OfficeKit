# What-kind: Route and Scenario Contract

Choose exactly one primary scenario and at most one secondary scenario:

- `analysis-decision`: compare evidence and make a decision;
- `management-report`: align leaders around status, trade-offs, and action;
- `technical-engineering`: explain systems, constraints, and causal paths;
- `academic-research`: establish method, evidence, uncertainty, and result;
- `education-training`: teach a sequence, model, or transferable action;
- `brand-creative`: create recognition, desire, and a memorable reveal.

Choose one design source in this order:

1. user-provided brand/design system;
2. selected template or reference deck;
3. style transfer from an explicitly supplied visual reference;
4. self-directed direction when no authority exists.

Then choose `live`, `reader`, or `hybrid`. Scenario and design source explain
the communication context; they do not prescribe a fixed layout, palette, or
component inventory.

The scenario guide is a decision aid. Read only the selected guide under
`references/scenarios/`; do not load all scenario files by default.
