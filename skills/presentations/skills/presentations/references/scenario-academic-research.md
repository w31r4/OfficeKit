# Scenario: academic research

## Audience task

Help an audience evaluate a question, method, evidence, uncertainty, and
contribution without overstating the result.

## Narrative

Establish the question and prior gap, explain the method and data, present the
result, test alternatives or limitations, interpret cautiously, and state the
contribution and next inquiry.

## Density and rhythm

Use deliberate progression and consistent analytical views. Give methods and
uncertainty enough room to be inspectable. Use sparse synthesis pages between
dense evidence sequences.

## Visual carriers and archetypes

- research question and conceptual model;
- method or study-flow diagram;
- annotated figure with comparable scales;
- result plus uncertainty interval;
- robustness or limitation comparison;
- contribution and open-questions synthesis.

## Visual grammar

Use stable notation, quiet surfaces, precise lines, figure-first layouts, and
highly legible labels. Color should encode variables or confidence consistently.
Citations belong near the claim or figure they support. Decoration must never
compete with data.

## Avoid

Decorative science imagery, causal language for exploratory evidence, hidden
denominators, inconsistent notation, unreadable figure imports, oversized
conclusions, and references detached from their claims.

## Review questions

- Can the audience distinguish question, method, result, and interpretation?
- Are uncertainty, limitations, and provenance visible at the point of use?
- Do comparable figures share notation and scales?
- Does the conclusion stay within what the evidence supports?
