# Tidal Research

Use this style when field study. Field evidence is organized by tidal lines and observation windows.

## Visual premise

- Build the page around the **tide** motif; repeat it sparingly so the deck feels related without repeating one layout.
- Use **#FBFCFA** as the main field, **#17352F** for primary type, **#188A78** for the decisive signal, **#58AEE6** for secondary evidence, and **#E1B84D** for rules or context.
- Keep density **medium**. a rigorous display/body hierarchy with precise figure labels.
- figures keep units, source context, and the decisive region visible.
- Use documentary wetland imagery as evidence or atmosphere; crop decisively and keep typography independent from busy detail.

## Composition

- Give each page one dominant reading path and vary the silhouette between cover, analysis, data, and decision pages.
- Use rules, axes, scale changes, and direct labels before reaching for repeated containers.
- Let negative space isolate the claim; do not leave space that has no focusing or pacing role.

## Avoid

- Do not use this style for consumer launch.
- Do not turn every fact into an equal card or badge.
- Do not add decorative motifs that compete with the evidence.

## Calibration

The examples are original fictional calibration pages. Recompose every real slide from its communication task; do not copy their coordinates or text.
