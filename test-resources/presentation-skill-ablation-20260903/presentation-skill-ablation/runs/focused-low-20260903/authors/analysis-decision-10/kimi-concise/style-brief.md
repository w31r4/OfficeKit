# Task-local style brief

Goal: Help a reader see the supplied endpoint change and its label clearly while preserving the source chart’s meaning and unrelated content.

Visual thesis: The endpoint is the single point of attention; clear clearance around its label makes the change legible without changing the chart’s evidence.

Primary carrier: chart

Visual move: Keep the existing bar-line chart and re-anchor only the colliding endpoint label/callout and its immediate layer relationship, preserving the source chart scale, categories, and styling.

Reading order: 1 → 2 → 3 — chart title/context; bars and line evidence; endpoint metric and label as the focused annotation. Existing source context remains secondary.

Visual DNA: Preserve the template’s existing dark field, restrained chart palette, type hierarchy, geometry, and line language. Use native chart labels and true back-to-front order; no new imagery or decorative motif.

Avoid: rebuilding the chart, adding cards or badges, changing scales or non-target data, inventing an intermediate trend, or covering bars, line, labels, or source text with a mask.

Review probes: endpoint label/value collision with the line or bar; line/marker visibility above bars; preservation of the adjacent page and source-bound opaque content after re-import.
