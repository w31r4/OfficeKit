# Market Trends Report

## Communication territory

Use this style for an industry or market report that turns noisy signals into
a time-bounded view of what changed, why it changed, and what should be
watched next. It works for investors, strategy teams, and research readers.
It is a poor fit for a purely operational weekly scorecard or a brand story.

## Visual grammar

Start with a dark, quiet opening field and a restrained warm accent for the
one signal that changes the market reading. Interior evidence pages may move
to an almost-white field with charcoal type and one saturated trend color.
Keep the color role stable: the accent marks the selected series or turning
point, while all comparison series remain neutral. Use generous type scale,
short labels, thin axis rules, and direct annotations instead of legends that
force the reader to hunt.

## Page vocabulary

1. **Market thesis:** one claim, the observation window, and the implication.
2. **Signal timeline:** a real time axis with only the events that explain the
   movement; annotate the inflection instead of decorating it.
3. **Driver comparison:** two or three forces on a shared basis, with a small
   method note.
4. **Scenario frame:** an explicit base, upside, and downside with triggers
   and observation indicators.
5. **Action close:** what to monitor, who owns the next read, and when the
   view will be revisited.

Use charts, small multiples, and annotated tables to carry the argument.
Reserve photography for a sector or place context. Do not fill a sparse page
with generic finance imagery or a wall of rounded cards.

## Layers and evidence

When an image leads a page, use a native background or an ordered image
element, then one contrast field, then editable title and evidence. Labels,
markers, confidence bands, and trend lines must stay unobscured after render.
Every series declares unit, date range, source, and whether it is observed or
an assumption. A dashed line is meaningful only when its basis is explained.

## Review

Check that the title states the market conclusion, the chart scale does not
hide volatility, and scenarios have observable triggers rather than invented
precision. Keep one base transition and only use motion to reveal chronology
or comparison. The bundled PPJ/PPTX is a source-bound Codex migration for
native inspection; it is evidence of the visual direction, not a fixed page
clone. Recompose every new page and re-import before delivery.
