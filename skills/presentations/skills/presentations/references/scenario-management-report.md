# Scenario: management report

## Audience task

Help leaders understand what changed, why it changed, what needs attention, who
owns the response, and what decision or alignment is required.

## Narrative

Lead with the operating headline, then move through material outcomes, drivers,
exceptions, interventions, owners, and forward risks. Organize around decisions
and variance, not around the reporting org chart.

## Density and rhythm

Use an executive summary followed by evidence pages of controlled density.
Alternate performance views with driver or action pages. Reserve sparse pages
for decisions, escalations, and section turns.

## Visual carriers and archetypes

- headline plus compact score strip;
- actual-versus-plan trend with annotated inflections;
- driver decomposition;
- exception table with owner and due date;
- operating flow or dependency map;
- forward outlook with triggers and accountable actions.

## Visual grammar

Use disciplined alignment, stable metric colors, tabular rhythm, thin rules,
and rectangular regions with clear hierarchy. Distinguish status, variance,
and action roles; do not reuse one accent color for all three. Use restrained
rounding only where a grouped surface actually improves reading.

## Avoid

KPI card walls, traffic-light color without thresholds, tiny footnotes, repeated
three-column templates, activity reports without outcomes, and ownerless action
lists.

## Review questions

- Can a leader identify the material change in under one minute?
- Are drivers separated from symptoms and activities?
- Do exceptions lead to an owner, date, or decision?
- Can the deck serve as a durable record after the meeting?
