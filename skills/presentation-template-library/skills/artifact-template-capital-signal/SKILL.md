# Capital Signal

## Communication territory

A dark market terminal translated into a cinematic briefing, with gold reserved for the decisive signal.

Use this style for capital markets outlook, fintech investor briefing, risk asset narrative. Avoid it for children education, pastel wellness story.

This is a visual direction, not a page library. Read the calibration images for hierarchy, rhythm, and relationships. Build every slide from the current content and do not trace sample geometry or reuse sample wording.

## Design grammar

- **Composition:** Build depth with a quiet field, one luminous trajectory, disciplined labels, and large numeric anchors.
- **Typography:** Wide sans-serif headlines and monospaced data labels separate interpretation from measurement.
- **Geometry and surface:** Use hairline grids, open plotting fields, clipped arcs, and sparse coordinates rather than framed boxes.
- **Evidence:** Show a real time axis, baseline, uncertainty band, or scenario threshold; label only the values that change the decision.
- **Imagery:** Use atmospheric macro photography or material textures at low contrast; never use generic trading-floor stock art.
- **Recurring motif:** One gold signal line may travel across adjacent pages and change role with the narrative.
- **Density:** Use a medium default, then vary it intentionally between orientation, proof, and decision pages.
- **Palette roles:** Treat #080B12 as the base field, #F2E5B5 as primary ink, #D7A928 as the decisive accent, #46B5A7 as supporting evidence, and #465168 as quiet structure. Adapt values when a user design system is authoritative.

## Page behavior

- Give every page one audience task, one claim, and one primary visual carrier.
- Derive new page silhouettes from this grammar. Do not repeat one arrangement across the deck.
- Keep charts, diagrams, images, and typography in one information relationship; do not place them as independent decorations.
- Use native editable PowerPoint objects and licensed or user-provided imagery. Render and review every page.

## Avoid

- Neon overload.
- Gold on every object.
- Fake terminal decoration.
- Do not mix another template's visual language. A user-provided design system or reference deck remains authoritative.
- Do not copy the calibration text or coordinates; they demonstrate behavior, not reusable pages.
