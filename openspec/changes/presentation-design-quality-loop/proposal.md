## Why

The 0.9 Strategy Compiler gives OfficeKit a communication doctrine, seven
scenarios, and deck-specific design grammar, but stable visual quality still
depends too heavily on whether the Agent turns those principles into concrete
page decisions. Recent dogfood improved substantially while still exposing
generic chart surfaces, weak asset planning, and uneven translation from a
chosen direction to the full deck.

## What Changes

- Deepen the seven clean-room scenario guides with concrete decision aids for
  evidence hierarchy, page archetypes, visual carriers, chart/table treatment,
  imagery, density rhythm, line/surface rules, and scenario-specific failure
  modes. These remain generative rules, not fixed templates or copied designs.
- Add a calibration spread to the existing C route: compose and render three
  representative pages (opening, evidence/data, and the densest or most complex
  page), reconcile the design grammar, then complete the deck without asking the
  user to choose internal layouts.
- Require each planned visual carrier to name its source strategy: user asset,
  template/reference asset, sourced image, generated image, chart/diagram, or
  native composition. Missing optional image tools selects another honest
  carrier instead of producing a decorative placeholder.
- Add reusable, public-API examples for de-defaulted charts, editorial rules,
  image crops, diagrams, and asymmetric compositions. Examples demonstrate
  decisions and relationships; they do not become a hidden layout library.
- Separate authoring and review instructions. The reviewer receives the brief,
  plan, grammar, and render evidence, but does not reuse the page-generation
  rationale as proof that the design succeeded.
- Use a small set of unseen real briefs to check generalization. No A/B/C rerun,
  benchmark matrix, fixed palette mandate, or aesthetic score is introduced.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `presentation-design-guidance`: Clean-room, scenario-specific design decision
  guidance and reusable examples that remain free of fixed house styles.
- `presentation-design-calibration`: A three-page calibration and independent
  render-review loop before full-deck completion.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Updates the Presentations Skill, seven scenario references, shared visual
  guidance, create/review tasks, examples, and authoring-plan documentation.
- Reuses the current authoring plan, Compose primitives, render/inspect/review,
  task resume, and Help index. Runtime APIs and Office wire protocol remain
  unchanged unless real dogfood proves a missing deterministic primitive.
- Adds only minimal Skill/package assertions and three one-off unseen dogfood
  records; it does not add a permanent evaluation harness or release gate.
