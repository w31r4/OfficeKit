# Business Review

## Communication territory

Use this direction for a recurring executive review that must move from the
quarter's headline to the operating evidence, the causes of variance, and the
few decisions that follow. It serves leaders who need to scan first and ask
for detail second. It is not a generic strategy deck and it should not turn
every metric into a dashboard tile.

## Visual grammar

Keep a cool near-white field, a single confident blue for the active signal,
deep navy for claims, and quiet slate for context and sources. Let a thin dark
rule establish the page edge; use short rules to separate evidence groups.
Prefer a large conclusion-led title, a one-line period or basis, and a
measured body scale. Numbers are large only when their unit and comparison are
immediately adjacent.

The page should feel like an editorial operating memo: broad margins, strong
left alignment, explicit axes, direct labels, and a visible reading order.
Use native charts, tables, and connectors for the evidence. A photo may ground
an opening or a people/market context page, but it never replaces the metric
that proves the claim.

## Page vocabulary

- **Headline read-through:** one conclusion, one period, and one decision
  implication before any supporting chart.
- **Performance field:** one dominant chart or ledger with direct labels and a
  short read-through at the edge of the evidence.
- **Variance bridge:** a real sequence of drivers from plan to actual; use a
  waterfall, linked line, or annotated bar rather than equal panels.
- **Portfolio or segment view:** compare a small number of meaningful groups
  on a shared baseline; keep the denominator and time basis visible.
- **Decision close:** owner, threshold, and next action on the same reading
  line as the final evidence.

Alternate dense evidence pages with a quieter statement page. Empty space is
reserved for the headline, a meaningful pause, or a clean chart edge; an
underfilled evidence field is not a style goal.

## Images, layers, and data

Use one image role per page: company context, customer scene, or section
pause. For a full-bleed image, the stack is native image background, tested
scrim, editable claim, then source and foreground data. Never allow a scrim,
bar, label, or line to cover another evidence mark. Use flat surfaces and
border-light tables; avoid card walls, colored side strips, random circles,
and decorative KPI boxes.

## Review

Check that every headline is a conclusion, every number has a period/unit,
plan and actual share a scale, and the final action is supported by the page
before it. Re-render and re-import the PPJ/PPTX. The included reference PPJ is
a source-bound migration of the historical Codex deck: inspect its native
objects when a user explicitly wants continuation, but compose new pages from
the current brief rather than cloning its wording or coordinates.
