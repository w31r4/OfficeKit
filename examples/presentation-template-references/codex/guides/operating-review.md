# Operating Review

## Communication territory

Use this direction for a weekly or monthly operating review where the reader
must see the current state, the variance, the owner, and the intervention in
one pass. It is strongest for service, product, and infrastructure teams with
recurring metrics. It should not be used to disguise missing evidence with a
pretty dashboard.

## Visual grammar

Use a deep navy opening or section field, a pale content field, light blue for
context, and one vivid signal color for the condition that needs attention.
The visual identity comes from a top rule, a disciplined baseline, and a
stable left rail for period and ownership—not from rounded containers. Use
large sans-serif conclusions, compact but readable body copy, and direct
labels beside their marks.

## Page vocabulary

- **Operating thesis:** the headline state and the threshold that defines it.
- **Metric spine:** a time series or aligned ledger with target, actual, and
  latest period in the same frame.
- **Exception field:** one variance with its driver, owner, and due date.
- **Dependency path:** a real sequence of handoffs or constraints drawn with
  connectors and named stages.
- **Action close:** three or fewer interventions, each tied to a measure.

Vary page silhouettes: a quiet title page, a dense evidence page, a process
page, and a decision page. Use tables for repeated rows and charts for change
over time. If a number is a status, show its rule and denominator.

## Layers and review

Image context is optional and has a declared job. If a photo occupies the
background, put the tested scrim below editable text and metrics. No line,
label, or status marker may be covered by an image or another annotation.
Avoid equal card grids, traffic-light color soup, pills, and decorative
circles. Review the rendered page at presentation size, then re-open the
PPJ/PPTX and confirm IDs, order, and source notes survive. The included
reference is a source-bound migration of the historical Codex operating deck;
use it for native continuation only when requested.
