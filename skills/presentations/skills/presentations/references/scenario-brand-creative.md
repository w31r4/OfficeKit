# Scenario: brand and creative

## Audience task

Help an audience remember a distinct promise, feel its relevance, and choose to
engage without sacrificing comprehension or native editability.

## Narrative

Create tension or desire, reveal the promise, show the product or idea in use,
prove the claim, expand the world, and end with a specific invitation. Use few
words and let image, scale, crop, and sequence carry meaning.

## Density and rhythm

Build strong contrast between reveal, detail, proof, and invitation. Sparse
pages are valid when the dominant visual earns the space. Follow an emotional
beat with concrete proof before moving on.

## Visual carriers and archetypes

- image-led opening with one memorable line;
- product or idea reveal;
- detail crop or material study;
- proof through demonstration, quote, or measured result;
- visual sequence or transformation;
- closing invitation with one action.

## Visual grammar

Choose one distinctive motif, a deliberate crop language, expressive scale,
and a controlled type system. Use SVG, imagery, native vectors, and negative
space as compositional material. Repetition should build recognition, not
substitute for invention. Motion may reveal or connect, but must not compensate
for generic static pages.

## Avoid

Stock-image filler, slogan stacks, gratuitous gradients, random icon clouds,
every page using the same hero crop, decorative circles without a role, and
visual novelty unrelated to the promise.

## Review questions

- Is the promise distinct, memorable, and supported by something concrete?
- Does the visual system feel authored for this idea rather than selected from a UI kit?
- Are image, typography, and motion carrying one coherent meaning?
- Is the final invitation specific and natural?
